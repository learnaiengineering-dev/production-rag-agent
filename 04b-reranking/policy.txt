Returns and refunds

Returning an item

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Start a return from the Orders page in your account. If your item is eligible you will see a prepaid label to print. Sale items can be returned within 14 days of delivery, not 30.

Faulty items

If an item arrives faulty, contact us within 90 days and we will replace it or refund it, whichever you prefer. We cover the return shipping for faulty items. Please include a photo of the fault, because it helps us check the problem with the supplier. Items damaged by misuse are not covered.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We issue the refund within 5 business days of receiving your item at our warehouse. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. Gift card purchases are refunded as store credit, and store credit never expires.

Exchanges

To swap an item for a different size or colour, start a return and place a new order. We do not hold stock for exchanges. Exchanged items that arrive after the 30 day window are not accepted.

Items we cannot take back

We cannot accept returns of opened hygiene products, personalised items, or gift cards. Perishable goods such as food and fuel canisters are also excluded. If you are unsure, contact us before you send anything back.
