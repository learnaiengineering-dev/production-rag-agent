Agreement with the human labels (12 items):
  exact match : 6/12 = 50%  (false pass 0, false fail 6)
  stand-in    : 11/12 = 92%  (false pass 1, false fail 0)

Reading it:
- Exact match fails most correct paraphrases: high false-fail.
- The stand-in judge agrees more, but still lets wrong answers pass
  (false-pass) when they happen to contain the right words.
- Whatever the judge, the only number that matters first is its
  agreement with YOUR labels. A judge you have not measured is a guess.
