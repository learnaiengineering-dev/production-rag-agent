Day 14 - feedback that tells you why
========================================================

What a thumbs-only system gives you:
  8 of 20 answers got a thumbs-down (40%).
  Something is wrong 40% of the time. What? No idea.

What a reason you can count gives you:
   4  missing_source -> retrieval & citations
   2  wrong_fact     -> the source data (or retrieval)
   1  wrong_format   -> output parsing / schema
   1  outdated       -> re-indexing / freshness

  Top reason: missing_source (4 of 8 downvotes) -> fix retrieval & citations.
  Now you know where to look first.

Why the reason must be an enum, not free text:
  Same 8 downvotes, stored as free text: 8 different strings - nothing to count.
  Stored as an enum: 4 buckets you can rank.
